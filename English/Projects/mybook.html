Brief

As part of Strasbourg Capitale Mondiale du Livre 2024 (Strasbourg World Capital of Books 2024), to encourage young people to read, we were asked to design an Adobe XD app prototype with the features described above.

We first researched references of similar apps, then created our own zoning and wireframes for MyBook, before adapting and prototyping them on Adobe XD.
